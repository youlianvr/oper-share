# Grill Me

> Reworked per owner verdict 2026-09-19: the vendor "one question at a time"
> discipline was wrong for this workspace. The job is **maximum coverage** —
> get every question out, grouped by branch, then walk the answers. What
> survives from the original: walk the tree depth-first, explore the
> codebase before asking what it can answer, always attach a recommended
> answer.

## The job

Interview the owner about a plan or design until shared understanding is
reached. **Coverage is the metric**: an unasked question is a decision made
silently later. Resolving one branch before opening the next still holds
(dependency order), but questions within a branch are batched.

## Mechanics

1. **Explore first.** Anything answerable from the codebase, the workspace
   indexes, or prior owner verdicts — resolve it yourself and state the
   finding as an assumption for confirmation, not a question.
2. **Extract the decision tree.** Walk the plan and list every branch:
   scope, data, behavior, edge cases, failure modes, tooling, rollout,
   rollback, what is explicitly out of scope.
3. **Batch by branch** through `ask_questions` (up to 4 questions per call):
   each question carries a one-line recommended answer with rationale.
   Unresolved branches spawn the next batch. A plan of any real size takes
   several batches — that is success, not friction.
4. **Record as you go.** Confirmed answers land in the plan file (or the
   current plan record) in minimal paraphrased form — the same turn, not
   batched at the end. Exact-recording discipline: enough clarifying
   questions, then state your version of the understanding; write it down
   only once the owner confirms.
5. **Stop when coverage is complete**: every branch resolved or explicitly
   deferred by the owner; no silent assumptions left. Then summarize the
   locked decisions and open deferrals.

## What this skill is not

- No CONTEXT.md / ADR ceremony (the vendor docs-anchored mode assumed files
  this workspace does not keep; decision records go to the plan file).
- No session-tracker scripts — the archived vendor scripts are gone;
  `ask_questions` and the plan file are the whole machinery.
- Not a substitute for AGENTS.md step 6: during execution, questions come
  at the moment a deviation appears. Grill-me is for the **plan** stage,
  up front, where the owner explicitly wants the full interrogation.

## When to use

- The owner says "grill me" / "stress-test this plan" / "прожарь меня".
- A large plan is about to be locked and the owner wants every assumption
  surfaced before work starts.
